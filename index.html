Questions

Plainly answered.

Is Ekaaki a bank or a lender?

No. Ekaaki is a referral and distribution service, not a bank, lender, insurer or fund house. We don't lend, underwrite or manage money. We match your profile to partnered banks, NBFCs, insurers and fund houses, prepare your file, and the provider makes the final decision.

What can you arrange?

Secured and unsecured loans (personal, business, professional, home and loan against property), insurance, credit cards and mutual funds — all sourced from a panel of banks, NBFCs, insurers and fund houses.

Do you charge a fee?

No. We earn a referral or distribution fee from the provider only when you go ahead, so our service is free to you.

Is approval guaranteed?

No. Eligibility, rates, premiums and terms are decided solely by the provider. We tell you where you stand before you commit, so there are no surprises.
